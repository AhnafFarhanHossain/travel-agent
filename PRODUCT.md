# AI Travel Agent

## Register

product

## Users

Anybody planning a trip — from spontaneous weekend explorers to methodical itinerary-builders. They arrive curious about destinations and leave with a clear, personalized plan.

## Product Purpose

An AI-powered travel planning tool that turns the chaos of trip research into a confident, inspiring, and efficient experience. Success looks like users discovering places they'd never considered and having a complete itinerary they trust.

## Brand Personality

**Confident, Warm, Curious.** Expert guidance delivered without jargon. The tone is knowledgeable but approachable — like a well-traveled friend who's genuinely excited to share their finds.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated slop patterns (gradient text, glassmorphism, side-stripe borders, repeating stripe backgrounds, decorative grid overlays)
- Travel-industry clichés (excessive map motifs, palm-tree iconography, postcard filters)
- Over-polished stock-photo design that lacks real character

## Design Principles

1. **Inspiration first, logistics second.** The interface should spark wanderlust before it asks for dates and budgets. Discovery leads; forms follow.
2. **Confidence through clarity.** Every screen answers "what do I do next?" and "why should I trust this recommendation?" No ambiguous states, no dead ends.
3. **Warmth without weight.** The UI is approachable and human (warm tones, rounded but not pillowy) without sacrificing information density or performance.
4. **System over one-offs.** A coherent token system and component library ensure the product feels designed, not assembled. Every new feature extends the system rather than inventing new patterns.
5. **Anticipate, don't interrogate.** Surface relevant options before the user asks. Reduce typing, increase choosing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

General good practices: semantic HTML, sufficient color contrast, keyboard-navigable, screen-reader friendly. No formal WCAG certification target but no known barriers accepted.
